I’m writing this because I feel like I don’t have any joy anymore in terms of programming;;;;

why...

as you expected, we already live in the AI era. No matter where you work, the company forces you to use AI; more product == more profit, more tasks that can be completed == more productive, there’s nothing wrong with that

but...

in the beginning, when I was learning programming languages back in high school, printing a pyramid shape of * using a for loop was my first joy of coding. It was like solving a puzzle and being able to complete it. That’s why becoming a software engineer was my dream job;

reading the documentation, asking on forums and pasting the error code into Google search felt like being a detective trying to solve the puzzle, piece by piece, and assembling it together.

did I regret my decision to become a software engineer? yes... that’s the simplest answer I can give so far;

When I'm writing this, I still don't know which career I should move towards. Should I just accept that AI will replace me??? Or become an "AI Engineer"? It feels like this position will soon be replaced by "AI that becomes the AI Engineer";

I hope that soon I'll know which direction I should lean towards;
